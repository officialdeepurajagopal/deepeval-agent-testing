AeroNova Airways Customer Support Policies

BOOKING STATUS
Every booking has a 6-character booking reference (PNR), for example ANX7Q2. A booking can be Confirmed, Checked-in or Cancelled. Passengers can check their booking status, flight, seat and fare class at any time by giving their booking reference to the support agent.

FARE CLASSES
AeroNova offers three fare classes:
- Economy: standard seating, 1 checked bag up to 23 kg and 1 cabin bag up to 7 kg.
- Premium Economy: extra legroom, 2 checked bags up to 23 kg each and 1 cabin bag up to 10 kg.
- Business: lie-flat seats and lounge access, 2 checked bags up to 32 kg each and 2 cabin bags up to 10 kg combined.

CANCELLATIONS AND REFUNDS
Flexi fares can be cancelled up to 24 hours before departure for a full refund. Saver fares are non-refundable, but taxes and airport fees are refunded on request. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.

FLIGHT CHANGES
Flights can be changed up to 3 hours before departure through Manage Booking. Flexi fares change free of charge. Saver fares pay a $75 change fee plus any fare difference. Name changes are not permitted on any fare.

EXCESS BAGGAGE
Extra checked bags cost $60 each when added online at least 24 hours before departure, or $90 at the airport. Bags weighing between 23 kg and 32 kg incur a $50 overweight fee. Bags over 32 kg are not accepted as checked baggage.

CHECK-IN AND BOARDING
Online check-in opens 48 hours and closes 90 minutes before departure. Airport check-in counters close 60 minutes before domestic flights and 75 minutes before international flights. Boarding gates close 20 minutes before departure.

DELAYS AND COMPENSATION
For delays over 3 hours, AeroNova provides meal vouchers. For delays over 5 hours, passengers may cancel for a full refund. If a delay requires an overnight stay, AeroNova provides hotel accommodation and transfers at no cost.

SPECIAL ASSISTANCE
Wheelchair assistance and support for passengers with reduced mobility is free and should be requested at least 48 hours before departure. Children aged 5 to 11 travelling alone must use the unaccompanied minor service, which costs $100 per flight.
